# Camera position, 2026-09-26

`--calibrate` prints the old OpenCV smile fraction and the Straight Face frame-difference. It does not tune Presage, and a live face round does not use those numbers. The pre-demo check is where the face sits in the Brio frame.

An 8 second read of camera 2 (Logitech Brio 101, 640x480), with the rig as it was sitting, produced these Presage validation hints in order:

- `0` Hold still and record
- `17` Face the camera
- `11` Use a camera mode that provides at least 25 frames per second
- `15` Move down, or tilt the camera up

Haar saw a face in 93% of frames. Presage still returned composure samples, but they sat around 2, and the last hint was code 15: the face was too high in the frame. That is not a locked-in good position. A usable window is one that stays on hint `0` (hold still) without flipping to `15` (too high) or `16` (too low). Tilt the camera up, or have the player sit lower, until the chin and the top of the head are both inside the picture.

The detector on this machine runs around 15 fps. Presage asked for 25. The scored Poker Face rounds still completed at about 15.5 fps, so the fps hint did not by itself reject a window.
